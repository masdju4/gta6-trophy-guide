# FAQ

Q: Does this app need the internet?
A: No. Open app/index.html directly from the files; nothing is fetched from the network.

Q: Where is my progress saved?
A: In this browser's localStorage. Clearing site data clears progress.

Q: How do I move progress to another browser?
A: Use Export to save a small JSON file, then Import it in the other browser.

Q: What does "Hide story spoilers" do?
A: It blurs the names, descriptions and guides of story trophies until you earn them.

Q: What counts as a missable trophy?
A: A trophy tied to a named mission, choice, collection or count in the official description. Replay or sweep before moving on.

Q: Are the guides copied from other sites?
A: No. They are written for this entry and each one carries a source line.

Q: Is this affiliated with Rockstar?
A: No. It is a fan-made guide with original layout and no official artwork.
