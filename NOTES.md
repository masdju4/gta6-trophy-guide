# Method and sources

The trophy names, descriptions and grades are the official PlayStation 5 list supplied by the kit. Public cross-checks were GTA Wiki, GTA6 Field Guide, Leonida Interactive and the Exophase/PSNProfiles lists used before the bounty opened.

Roadmap stages are derived from the official descriptions: campaign chapters first, then heists and major arcs, then stranger strands, collectibles and properties, and finally activities and challenges. Platinum is the last stage because it requires the other 54.

Missable flags are derived from descriptions that name a specific mission, choice, collection or count. Story missions and choice-based trophies can be missed by advancing too far; collectible and count trophies need a deliberate sweep.

Every guide is written in this entry's own words. It explains the condition in the official description and a sensible way to approach it. A source line accompanies every guide; no guide copies another site's prose.

The app loads only local files. Progress is stored in the browser and can be exported or imported as a small JSON file.

No Rockstar Games logo, official artwork or game screenshot is used. The visual identity is original.
