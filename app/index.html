Sources & method

Official trophy names, descriptions and grades come from the PlayStation 5 trophy list supplied with this kit. Cross-checks: GTA Wiki achievements list, GTA6 Field Guide trophy page, Leonida Interactive trophy guide, and the Exophase/PSNProfiles lists used before this bounty opened.

Every guide on the checklist is written in this entry's own words. It explains what the official description asks for and how to approach it; it does not copy another guide. Each guide carries its source line beside it. The app never loads anything from the network: all 55 records live in data/trophies.json beside the page.

Progress is stored in this browser only. Export writes a small JSON file; import restores it. No account, upload or tracking is involved.
